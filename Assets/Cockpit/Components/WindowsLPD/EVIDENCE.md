# Evidence audit — 2026-09-08

This is an approximate flight-window visual reconstruction, not surveyed LM-5 optics.

- NASA TN D-7439, printed pp8–10, local PDF pp13–15: forward windows have two panes, a vented cavity, flat oblique triangular geometry (nominal sides 25/28/24 inches); outer pane protective, inner structural. Printed p10 specifies black velvet paint around pane periphery, conductive defog coating on outboard face of inner pane, HEA on inboard faces, BR coating on outer face of outer pane. BR is a spectral coating, not red/blue LPD ink. Source: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19730023038/downloads/19730023038.pdf
- Grumman course30915 T30915-38, January1967, printed p36/original PDF p44: rounded triangular window and inner/outer retainers; LPD diagram explicitly LOOKING INBOARD and thus lettering mirrored from the crew view. Upper crossbar and 0–60 descending scale shown. Monochrome scan cannot establish ink color. This LM-2-era training basis is not automatically an LM-5 production drawing. T30915-39 printed p35/original p43 establishes design-eye body stations; rays read from its plot are not dimensioned corners. Source: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/lm_structures_study_guide.pdf
- NASA-hosted Eppler presentation, slide38 of154, actual window photograph at lower right: pale warm/cream-gold thin marks, duplicated with parallax, crossbars at approximately 0 AND50, readable 0–60 numerals. Vehicle/mission and original photographer are not identified on this slide; exact ink color cannot be separated from illumination/coatings. The cyan-filled diagram is an illustration, not glass color measurement. Presentation text calls both panes pressure panes, conflicting with structural report; use TN D-7439 for construction. Source: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/eppler-slides-apollo-lunar-landing-experience-report-20070-r4.pdf
- AS11-36-5389HR, Apollo11 LM-5 in flight: LMP-side window rounded corners, substantial layered perimeter, restrained dark rim and pale metallic retention. LMP equipment crosses view; not modeled as window hardware. Source mirror: https://apollojournals.org/alsj/a11/AS11-36-5389HR.jpg (former NASA static URL returned404 in source archive). 69-H-134 Apollo11 preflight photograph supplies complementary frame arrangement: https://apollojournals.org/alsj/a11/ap11-69-H-134.jpg

## Consequences

Previous BASELINE-CommanderWindowCalibration.md claim that the lower bar is merely the duplicated zero bar is contradicted by the newer actual-window photograph. Baseline code uses a single zero crossbar and diagnostic saturated paired colors in its presentation. Retain baseline for migration, not authority. New visual artwork uses 0 and50 crossbars and physically attached nonemissive pale warm marks on both CDR panes. No LMP LPD, no floating digital numeric indicator. Any app diagnostic palette belongs exclusively to optional training UI; do not bake it into normal hardware.

Exact RGB, roughness, .65mm mark width, 7mm numeral height, frame widths, fastener spacing, 3mm glass thickness, 20mm cavity, corner trim and assigned docking pose are visual choices. Pale warm material approximates the observed photo under neutral light, not a claim of historical gold pigment. No calibrated photometry, pane survey, stereo or headset acceptance.

Retain old eye and sharp triangle as agreed provisional construction envelope to avoid substituting a different unsupported fit.
