# Source interpretation and limits

Source snapshots and SHA-256/byte counts: `provenance.json`. No crop was used: the retained Apollo images and DSKY outline are complete source rasters. The companion manifests/catalog retain original URLs, including failed NASA migration URLs and the working Apollo Journals archive copies. No newly downloaded source or unrecorded scale extraction was used.

Apollo 11 AS11-36-5389 supplies the mission-led forward-panel surface and instrument-surround relationship. It prominently shows Panel 2/LMP detail; its symmetry is not assumed to prove CDR dimensions. Preflight 69-H-134 supplies direct commander-window/panel layered-edge context, but is perspective, partially obscured, monochrome and not a mounting survey. Both support independent sheet, trim and fastener forms. Neither establishes this rectangular outline, thickness, aperture or mounting hardware. Glare shields stay omitted as in the accepted optional assembly. No labels or switch catalog is invented.

DSKY outline 2003956 Rev B, archive PDF page 73 (see `DSKY/sources.json` and full raster) establishes front envelope 8.124 x 8 in and thread-center X pitch 7.700 in. The earlier DSKY audit distinguishes the 6.900 in shoulder reference and 2.000 in REF front-to-mount relationship. Its current .19558 m visual rear-box width puts the thread axes exactly on the box side boundaries: zero radial room at the centerline. A threaded receiving flange through that box cannot be inferred. The new surrounds therefore expose a conservative opening and an EMPTY interface instead of pretending to fasten the current box. Coordinator proposal: qualify the drawing/configuration-specific rear envelope and mounting plane before adding instrument fasteners or reducing these gaps; leave instrument poses unchanged during that work.

FDAI has no verified LM-5 mechanical dimensions in the delivered evidence. Its provisional 5.75 in face derives from early unflown hardware listing evidence; Apollo 14 supplies some delivered trim appearance. Those later/early substitutions remain inherited reference limitations, not a claim that this support is LM-5 hardware. No later-mission geometry was newly copied into these surrounds.

The full Panel 1 reservation trial generated 23 intersecting mesh pairs against fixed Cabin optics/supports. This is computational evidence of an incompatible reservation, not a flaw in the optical calibration. Narrowing the NEW outboard outline 85 mm resolves all detected surface intersections while preserving datums. The actual attachment from these channel returns into the Cabin structure is not engineered: no undocumented brackets bridge remaining gaps. Cabin mount nodes are registration interfaces only. Panel 4 outline and both instruments retain accepted poses.

Documented dimensions: DSKY drawing front envelope and mounting pitch. Inherited inferred dimensions: Cabin panel reservations/cants and FDAI exterior. Deliberate provisional allowances: 3 mm per side full-envelope aperture, 85 mm Panel 1 outline reduction, backing/channel/collar/head geometry. Computational observations: current artifact bounds, clearance lower bounds and surface intersection results. None of these observations certifies flight fit, load transfer, insertion path, cable bend radius, hand reach, optics occlusion over a head box, or egress.
