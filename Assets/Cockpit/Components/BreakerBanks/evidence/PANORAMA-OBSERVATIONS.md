# LM-2 interactive panorama review

Viewed 2026-09-08 in the Codex in-app browser, including forward instrument stack, both side stations, overhead lining and lower/aft equipment. Source: [Smithsonian panorama](https://airandspace.si.edu/multimedia-gallery/panorama/lm-interiorjpg), [interactive viewer](https://airandspace.si.edu/explore-and-learn/multimedia/vr?passthrough=/files_special/Panos/A19711598000_1). The visible page identifies LM-2 and lists rights as Contact Smithsonian Institution. LM-2 is a ground-test vehicle subsequently presented as Eagle; restoration/display state is not an LM-5 dimensional survey.

## Direct visual observations

- Forward panels form distinct planes: the tall central instrument stack, inclined lower control shelf, lower DSKY panel and separate side trays. Frames, exposed supports and cable routes create substantial depth between them.
- Side breaker banks form stepped terraces beneath the overhead lining. Their rows should eventually have individual depth/cant and supporting edges, rather than a single flat board carrying painted rows.
- Window openings have rounded triangular corners, layered perimeter bands, repeated fasteners and dark edge treatment. Reflective flexible covers/shades project into the cabin in this display. Do not mistake those for glazing or copy their deployed museum pose into descent configuration.
- The overhead includes pale cream solid shaped covers interrupted by mesh/perforated sections, visible seams, fasteners, openings and projecting fittings. It is not uniformly perforated and not a smooth featureless cylinder. Color here remains a photographic/restoration observation, not calibrated flight paint.
- Lower/aft space is occupied by bulky curved equipment housings, hoses/ducts, cable bundles and ribbed surfaces. This supports descriptive volume reservations while exact component identities remain unresolved.
- The forward view shows physical window surrounds and unpowered instruments. The featureless blue exterior/window field cannot establish glazing transparency, exact tint, LPD absence, or flight illumination. The panorama supplies no defensible new numerical eye datum or optical calibration.

## Application to the current foundation

Accept the closed shell and replaceable blank regions as a foundation. Their smooth liner, flat breaker-row reservations, simplified frame cross-sections and sparse supports are explicitly unfinished surface/detail work. Preserve current provisional mounting interfaces; pursue shaped ceiling liners, stepped breaker banks, shades, exposed supports and routed bundles as separate incremental components. Do not copy a simulated digital window indicator from the supplied collage.
