# Geometry review

`cabin-center.png` compares both surrounded openings in one interior view. `commander-eye.png`, `pilot-eye.png` and `commander-window-close.png` use the accepted crew-eye locations or the explicitly offset close-review camera. These previews include existing Cabin, WindowsLPD, CommanderPanels, PanelInventory and BreakerBanks as read-only context. They are not the final fully populated simulation assembly.

Broad structural fields replace the old cheek faces; glazing, LPD and original retainer hardware remain from WindowsLPD. Incidental triangulation normals are shared across the broad fields, while the actual reveal/fold boundaries remain crisp. The final closed-solid checks guard against reversed sheet winding. All cameras, lighting settings and hidden-glazing choices exist only in the transient review scene, not the delivered asset.

The central footprint and pilot-lamp reliefs were added after actual intersection/ray testing found exposed-front intrusions. Static breaker knobs were also checked and given backed mounting reliefs. The shared forward sheet boundary joins the unchanged Cabin and is not a new pressure-shell design. Fine sheet-metal radii, restored-photo texture, fasteners, exact gauge and surveyed dimensions remain future refinements. Runtime lighting and headset readability require the coordinator's combined native view.
