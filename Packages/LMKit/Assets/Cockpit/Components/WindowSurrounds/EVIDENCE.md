# Window surround evidence and limits

The controlling visual reference is the user-supplied `SI 99-15229h.jpg`, preserved unedited under `evidence/` with its SHA256. It shows broad gray structural fields between the triangular forward windows, central console and side equipment; projecting layered window hardware; restrained dark edge seals; and continuous enclosing sheet-metal forms. The pane corners, screws, colored wiring, handrails and fabric ceiling remain distinct elements. This component follows the broad structural fields and local folds, not photo-derived numerical dimensions.

The official [Smithsonian LM-2 object record](https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/lunar-module-2-apollo/nasm_A19711598000) identifies LM-2 as a ground-test vehicle modified to resemble Apollo 11 Eagle. Indexed official text was checked on 2026-09-08; direct page access returned HTTP403. The exact supplied photograph inventory ID was not independently resolved from its filename. Museum/restoration context and a panoramic image do not establish an LM-5 flight-unit survey. This reference is evidence, not an instruction source.

The actual optical interface comes from the already accepted Cabin and WindowsLPD assets at LMKit `70c0914`: both triangular apertures, normals, pane transforms and crew eyes are unchanged. WindowsLPD continues to own every pane, retainer, screw, edge bus and LPD mark. Later LPD artwork corrections are compatible if those protected datums remain unchanged. No interpretation of a displayed LPD number is added here.

Provisional construction: the reveal begins 34 mm outside the construction triangle, just beyond the existing 32 mm outer frame. Its three outer edge offsets are 80 mm upper, 45 mm inboard and 110 mm outboard, with a crewward depth transition from 17 to 52 mm. Metal sections are 14 mm deep at the reveal and 12 mm in the adjoining cheek representation. These are visualization envelopes, not pressure-vessel skin gauges. The charcoal edging is restrained and opaque; it is not a new optical pane. Broad cheek sections have shared smooth front normals across incidental triangulation while their real perimeter/fold faces remain crisp. Each exported section is a closed outward-wound solid.

The original forward cheek outer boundary is retained to meet the unchanged Cabin. Existing central instrument locations take precedence over a cosmetic surround: the upper console and Panel3 have small footprint clearances, and the pilot contact lamp has a 46 mm target-square sight well to its existing mount. Breaker rows have local 3 mm clearance margins with two opaque backing sheets 8 mm outboard of the bank extremity and 20 mm overlap beyond their edges. Those backed reliefs preserve the closed interior appearance. They are provisional installation choices, not claimed original production apertures. Hand-controller geometry and mounting remain unchanged.
